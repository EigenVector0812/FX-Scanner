Not investment advice. This tool only reports what standard technical indicators say about recent price action — it does not know your risk tolerance, account size, or news events. Indicators lag price and can conflict; treat a high "confluence score" as a prompt to research further, not a signal to trade.

Stop loss / take profit levels are mechanical: entry ≈ current price, stop = 1.5×ATR(14) against the position, target = 2.5×ATR(14) in its favor. ATR reflects the pair's recent typical range, not support/resistance, news risk, or your account size — treat these as a volatility-scaled starting point to adjust, not a level to follow blindly. Forex trading carries substantial risk of loss.
Setup
1. Get a free key at twelvedata.com (free tier: 8 requests/minute, 800/day — plenty for manual scans).
2. Paste it above and click Run scan. The key stays in this browser tab only — it's never saved or sent anywhere but Twelve Data.
3. Each scan pulls ~13 pairs on the 1-hour timeframe (100 candles) and computes indicators locally. Free-tier rate limits mean a full scan takes about 30–45 seconds — that's normal.
4. Re-run whenever you want a fresh read. There's no auto-refresh, to stay within free-tier limits.

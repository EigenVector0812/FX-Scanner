Not investment advice. This tool only reports what standard technical indicators say about recent price action — it does not know your risk tolerance, account size, or news events. Indicators lag price and can conflict; treat a high "confluence score" as a prompt to research further, not a signal to trade.

Stop loss / take profit levels are mechanical: entry ≈ current price, stop = 1.5×ATR(14) against the position, target = 2.5×ATR(14) in its favor. ATR reflects the pair's recent typical range, not support/resistance, news risk, or your account size — treat these as a volatility-scaled starting point to adjust, not a level to follow blindly.

Correlation adjustments are computed live from this scan's own price data (not a fixed textbook table), so they reflect current market behavior and will drift over time. A "Confirmed" badge means a strongly correlated pair currently agrees — it does not mean two independent confirmations; correlated pairs are often really one shared driver (e.g. broad USD strength) showing up twice. Forex trading carries substantial risk of loss.
